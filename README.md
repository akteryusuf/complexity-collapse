# The Complexity Collapse

Lecture notes on a proposed law relating structural complexity to energetic
variation, **K = 1/&Delta;E** — its geometry on an infinite funnel, the
finite-time collapse it implies, and the places the derivation does not hold.

By **Prof. Peter Copf**.

## Read it

**https://akteryusuf.github.io/complexity-collapse/**

Available in **English and Italian** — the `EN · IT` switch sits at the top of
the page. A reader's choice is remembered on their own device.

## Reviewing

- **The mathematics.** Section 13, *Open questions*, lists six problems in the
  model by name, including two the author states he cannot resolve. That
  section is the most useful place to start.
- **The Italian.** It has not yet been read by a native speaker with the
  physics background, which is why the Italian view carries a
  *"traduzione in revisione"* line. [`i18n/REVIEW-it.md`](i18n/REVIEW-it.md)
  sets the English beside the Italian, section by section, with the
  terminology table used throughout. Corrections belong in
  [`i18n/it.json`](i18n/it.json).

## Standing of the work

This is an **unrefereed proposal**. Nothing in it has been peer reviewed and it
should not be cited as established physics. The page says so itself, in
section 13.
